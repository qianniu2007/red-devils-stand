The 1999 Treble

Two minutes of stoppage time changed everything

On 26 May 1999 at the Camp Nou, the European Cup final. Trailing Bayern 1–0 after 90 minutes, Sheringham and Solskjær scored twice in stoppage time to win 2–1.

“Football, bloody hell.” — Ferguson’s words after the match, the line every Red Devils fan knows by heart.
